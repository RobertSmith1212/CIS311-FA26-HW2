Elm Street Food Pantry

Hours What We Need Volunteer FAQ

No one in Chester County should go hungry.

We distribute groceries every Tuesday and Saturday. No appointment, no paperwork, no questions about why you came.

Distribution Hours

Come to the fellowship hall door on the north side of the building. Parking is available in the gravel lot. If you need help carrying boxes to your car, just ask — that is what the volunteers in orange vests are there for.

Day Time Notes
Tuesday 4:00 – 7:00 p.m. Evening hours for working families
Saturday 8:00 – 11:00 a.m. Busiest day; fresh produce when available
First Sunday 12:30 – 2:00 p.m. Monthly diaper and hygiene distribution
Closed November 26 for Thanksgiving. Extra distribution added Tuesday, November 24.

What We Need Most

We can buy staples in bulk cheaply, so cash goes further than cans. But if you would rather bring food, these four items run out first every single week:

  • Peanut butter
  • Canned chicken and tuna
  • Diapers, sizes 4 and 5
  • Laundry detergent

Questions People Ask

Do I have to prove I need help?

No. We do not ask for income verification, ID, or a referral. Take what your household needs.

How often can I come?

Once a week. The monthly diaper distribution is separate and does not count against that.

Can I donate food that is past the "best by" date?

Shelf-stable items are usually fine past that date, but we cannot accept anything opened, home-canned, or refrigerated. When in doubt, call first.

Is this only for church members?

No. The pantry serves all of Chester County. You will never be asked where you worship.
